# SuwaLink MVP

## What is SuwaLink?

SuwaLink is a platform that connects patients and pharmacies, making it easy to find prescribed medicines without visiting or calling multiple pharmacies.

Instead of travelling from pharmacy to pharmacy trying to locate medicines, patients can search available medicines directly or upload a prescription and receive responses from nearby pharmacies that have the required items.

The MVP focuses on solving one simple but common problem:

> **Helping patients quickly find where their prescribed medicines are available.**

This creates value for both patients and pharmacies while laying the foundation for a larger digital healthcare ecosystem.

---

# The Problem

Finding medicines in Sri Lanka is often frustrating.

Patients commonly experience situations where:

- They cannot read their doctor's handwriting.
- They do not know which pharmacy has their medicines.
- They spend hours travelling between pharmacies.
- They repeatedly call pharmacies asking about availability.
- Pharmacies lose potential customers simply because people don't know they have the medicine.

SuwaLink removes this friction by connecting both sides through a single platform.

---

# MVP Goal

The MVP is **NOT** trying to digitize healthcare.

The MVP is **NOT** replacing doctors.

The MVP is **NOT** introducing AI diagnosis.

The MVP solves only one problem exceptionally well:

> **Connecting patients with pharmacies to find prescribed medicines quickly.**

Everything else can be built on top of this foundation.

---

# Users

The MVP contains two primary user types.

## Patients

People searching for medicines.

They can:

- Register
- Login
- Search medicines
- Upload prescriptions
- Receive pharmacy responses
- Reserve medicines

---

## Pharmacies

Registered pharmacies that maintain their inventory.

They can:

- Register
- Login
- Manage inventory
- Receive prescription requests
- Respond with available medicines
- Reserve medicines for customers

---

# Core Features

---

## Patient Registration & Login

Patients can create an account using basic information.

This allows them to:

- Save prescription history
- View previous requests
- Track reservations
- Receive notifications

---

## Pharmacy Registration & Login

Pharmacies register with their business information.

Once approved they gain access to:

- Inventory Management
- Prescription Requests
- Reservation Management
- Notifications

---

# Pharmacy Inventory Management

Each pharmacy maintains its own medicine inventory.

They can:

- Add medicines
- Edit medicines
- Remove medicines
- Update stock quantity
- Update pricing
- Mark items unavailable

The inventory acts as the source when responding to prescription requests.

This eliminates manually typing medicine names every time.

---

# Medicine Search

Patients can search medicines exactly like searching products in an online store.

Examples:

- Panadol
- Amoxicillin
- Vitamin C

Search results display pharmacies currently offering that medicine.

Patients can compare:

- Pharmacy
- Price
- Availability

If the medicine can be found directly, no prescription upload is required.

---

# Prescription Upload System

If the patient cannot identify every medicine on the prescription or prefers to upload the prescription directly, they can create a Prescription Request.

The request includes:

- Prescription image
- Optional note
- Location (if required)

This request is then sent to nearby pharmacies.

---

# Prescription Notification System

Nearby pharmacies receive a real-time notification that a new prescription has been uploaded.

Instead of searching manually through social media or messaging apps, pharmacies receive organized requests directly inside SuwaLink.

---

# Pharmacy Response

A pharmacy reviews the uploaded prescription.

If they have matching medicines, they simply select products from their own inventory.

The response is linked directly to existing inventory items.

Each response may include:

- Available medicines
- Quantity
- Price
- Optional message

Multiple pharmacies can respond to the same prescription.

---

# Patient Notifications

Whenever a pharmacy responds, the patient receives a notification.

The patient can then view:

- Which pharmacy responded
- Which medicines are available
- Price
- Availability

This removes the need to visit multiple pharmacies.

---

# Reservation System

After reviewing responses, the patient can reserve medicines from the preferred pharmacy.

Reservations allow pharmacies to temporarily keep stock aside while the patient travels to collect it.

This increases confidence for both parties.

---

# Notification System

Notifications are an essential part of the MVP.

Patients receive notifications when:

- A pharmacy responds
- A reservation is confirmed
- Reservation status changes

Pharmacies receive notifications when:

- A new prescription request arrives
- A reservation is made
- Reservation is cancelled

---

# Typical Patient Journey

1. Register
2. Login
3. Search medicine

If found:

- Reserve from a pharmacy.

If not found:

- Upload prescription.
- Wait for pharmacy responses.
- Compare responses.
- Reserve medicines.

---

# Typical Pharmacy Journey

1. Register
2. Login
3. Maintain inventory.
4. Receive prescription requests.
5. Match medicines from inventory.
6. Respond to patient.
7. Receive reservation.
8. Prepare medicines for pickup.

---

# Why This MVP Matters

The MVP creates immediate value for both sides.

Patients save:

- Time
- Travel
- Phone calls
- Frustration

Pharmacies gain:

- New customers
- Better visibility
- Organized requests
- Digital inventory management

The platform becomes useful from day one without requiring hospitals, doctors, or government integration.

---

# Future Vision (Beyond MVP)

The MVP intentionally focuses on medicine discovery.

Once enough users and pharmacies are onboarded, SuwaLink can gradually evolve into a complete healthcare platform by introducing:

- Doctor registration
- Digital prescriptions
- Patient medical records
- Cross-hospital history
- AI-assisted prescription reading
- OCR
- Medicine recommendation models
- Inventory synchronization
- Pharmacy POS
- Delivery integration
- Online payments
- Healthcare analytics

These features are **not** part of the MVP but become significantly easier because the platform already connects patients and pharmacies.

---

# MVP Summary

SuwaLink is a marketplace connecting patients and pharmacies.

Patients can search medicines or upload prescriptions, while pharmacies manage inventory and respond with available medicines.

Patients receive real-time responses from multiple pharmacies, compare availability and pricing, and reserve medicines before visiting.

The MVP focuses on solving one everyday problem exceptionally well:

> **Helping patients quickly find and reserve their prescribed medicines from nearby pharmacies.**
